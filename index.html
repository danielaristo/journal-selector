📚 Journal Selector

Paste your article's abstract. The app searches Crossref (falling back to OpenAlex if unavailable) for thematically related work and identifies where it was published, cross-referenced with the Scimago (Scopus) ranking to show quartile, SJR and subject area.

⚠️ The quartile shown is SJR / Scopus (free, public data). The Web of Science JCR quartile requires a paid Clarivate subscription and is not available in this tool.
